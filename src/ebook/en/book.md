## Support

This book is free, and it will stay free. Behind it are more than thirty years of collecting, reading and writing, around 360 images and a glossary of about 760 terms.

If it is worth something to you, you can support it, once or regularly, with whatever amount suits you. Ko-fi takes PayPal, cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. It helps to keep kogakure.de running and the book going.

[Support it on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/kogakure)

You can also support the book without paying: pass it on and tell others about it.

## What’s in the book

The book holds the articles of this website, rewritten and set as a book, with a preface of its own. Each part opens with a painting and its name plate in brush script.

<figure>
<img src="/images/ebook/en/part.jpg" alt="A spread of the book: on the left a painting of riders on a mountain path, on the right the name plate 歴史 and the title “Part I, History”.">
<figcaption>The beginning of Part I</figcaption>
</figure>

Each chapter begins the same way: with its painting, its name plate and a short excerpt.

<figure>

## The formats

The **ebook** (EPUB) adapts to any screen. Read it on an e-reader or in a reading app, on a Kindle through <q>Send to Kindle</q>.

The **PDF** shows the pages as the book is set, with an index and links you can click. It is accessible (PDF/UA).
