The training of the ninja who practiced the **Togakure-Ryū** (戸隠流) covered eighteen systems. In each of them, they strove to improve. The levels range from knowing yourself through weapons and disguise to understanding weather and terrain.

## Mind, Body, and Weapons

1. _Seishin teki kyōyō_ (精神的教養): spiritual development
   - developing knowledge of yourself
   - knowing your strengths and weaknesses and the influences of others
   - examining your views on various things
2. _Taijutsu_ (体術): unarmed combat
   - _daken taijutsu_ (打拳体術): punches, kicks, blocks
   - _jū tai jutsu_ (柔体術): grappling techniques, chokeholds, escaping from holds
   - _taihen jutsu_ (体変術): moving quietly, rolling, jumping, falling techniques
3. _Ninja ken_: the ninja sword
   - _iai jutsu_ (居合術): quick drawing
   - _kenjutsu_ (剣術): fencing
4. _Bōjutsu_ (棒術): stick and staff
   - _bō_ (棒), the _roku shaku bō_
   - _jō_ (杖), the _yon shaku bō_
   - _hanbō_ (半棒), the _san shaku bō_
   - various sticks, such as the _tessen_ (鉄扇)
   - _shinobi zue_ (忍び杖): sticks with concealed weapons
5. _Shurikenjutsu_: throwing blades
   - _senban shuriken_: throwing stars
   - _bō shuriken_ (棒手裏剣): throwing knives
6. _Yarijutsu_ (槍術): the spear
   - _yari_ (槍): spear
   - _kama yari_ (鎌槍): hook spear
7. _Naginatajutsu_: halberds
   - _naginata_ (薙刀): sword lance
   - _bisentō_ (眉尖刀): heavy halberd
8. _Kusarigama_ (鎖鎌): chain and sickle weapons
   - _kusarigama_: chain and sickle
   - _kyoketsu shōge_ (距跋渉毛): rope and dagger

## In the Field

9. _Kayaku jutsu_ (火薬術): fire and explosives
   - manufacture, placement, timing
   - firearms
   - smoke and fire
10. _Hensō jutsu_ (変装術): disguise and acting
    - putting on costumes
    - thinking yourself into the role
    - adapting to the role
11. _Shinobi iri_ (忍び入り): stealth and entering fortifications
    - moving quietly
    - breaking in and breaking open
    - gaining access
    - running and walking techniques
    - creeping along the ground
    - using the shadows
12. _Bajutsu_ (馬術): riding
    - horseback riding
    - riding in armor in battle
13. _Suiren_ (水練): water training
    - silent swimming
    - silent movement in water
    - using boats
    - diving
    - underwater combat

## Strategy and Environment

14. _Bōryaku_ (謀略): strategy
    - tactics
    - battle formations
    - political plots
    - timing
    - influencing others
15. _Chōhō_ (諜報): espionage
    - espionage
    - training and recruiting spies
16. _Inton jutsu_ (隠遁術): escaping and hiding
    - _gotonpō_ (五遁法): using the five elements to escape
    - hiding
    - camouflage
17. _Tenmon_ (天文): weather science
    - weather forecasting
    - natural phenomena
18. _Chimon_ (地文): landscape studies
    - knowing and using the terrain
    - orientation
    - knowledge of vehicles
