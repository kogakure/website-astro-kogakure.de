Japanese houses were built of wood and paper, so fire was feared everywhere. The ninja put it to deliberate use: to spread panic and to see at night.

## Dōka

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/doka.webp" alt="A small, deeply patinated copper cylinder with a pierced sliding lid, charcoal smoldering inside.">
<figcaption><em>Dōka</em> (胴火)</figcaption>
</figure>

A piece of charcoal was always glowing inside the pocket heater _dōka_ (胴火). In winter it kept the ninja’s fingers warm, and the same embers could light a fuse.

## Gandō

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/gando.webp" alt="A cylindrical metal lantern open on one side, a candle mounted on a gimbal ring inside, casting a directed beam.">
<figcaption><em>Gandō</em> (龕灯)</figcaption>
</figure>

The lantern _gandō_ (龕灯) had one special feature: the candle inside was always held perpendicular to the ground.

## Kayaku Zutsu

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/kayaku-zutsu.webp" alt="Sealed bamboo tubes of varied length, waxed and bound with cord, some stoppered.">
<figcaption><em>Kayaku zutsu</em></figcaption>
</figure>

Sealed bamboo tubes let the ninja carry gunpowder and keep it dry; the name _kayaku zutsu_ (<q>powder tube</q>) is a modern description, not a recorded term. The <cite>Bansenshūkai</cite> (萬川集海) of 1676 lists more than 200 fire tools, and many of them ran on powder. The ninja used it to blast, to set fires, and to send smoke and light signals. Often the bang and the smoke alone were enough: they frightened the enemy and drew attention away from the ninja.

## Mizu Taimatsu

The _mizu taimatsu_ was a torch that kept burning even in the rain.

## Nin Shokudai

The _nin shokudai_ was an L-shaped torch that could be hung up.

## Tanagokoro

The palm torch _tanagokoro_ (掌) was especially useful when breaking open door locks.

## Uchi Dake

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/uchi-dake.webp" alt="A short bamboo tube with a fitted cap, faint smoke escaping, its interior charred black.">
<figcaption><em>Uchi dake</em></figcaption>
</figure>

The ninja carried embers in certain bamboo containers, the _uchi dake_. With them they could start a fire or light a lamp in moments.
