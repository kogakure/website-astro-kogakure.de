A ninja must be able to jump off a truck, leap over a high wall, or drop to the ground in a flash to dodge a bullet. That is what **_ukemi_** (受身) is for. The word means <q>falling</q> and covers every way of surviving a fall and reaching the ground unharmed. It also includes _kaiten_ (回転, <q>rolling</q>) and other body movements. The ninja uses these techniques when their legs are swept out from under them, when they want to get out of the opponent’s reach, or when they want to escape an attack.

Most martial arts neglect the skill of falling properly and springing straight back up to keep fighting or to flee, even though that is exactly what is needed. You don’t get to choose where you fight, unless you are the attacker – and that is **not** the point of ninjutsu. A fight can find you sitting in a train compartment, in a cramped broom closet, between the tables and chairs of a canteen, or on a staircase.

Practice starts in slow motion on a mat, from a deep squat. What matters is lowering your center of gravity, breathing out, and relaxing your body. Later you move to a meadow, then to hard ground, asphalt, stairs.

## Zenpō kaiten – Forward Roll

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/zenpo-kaiten.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in five phases: from a crouch he leans forward, places his arm, rolls over the shoulder, and comes back to a crouch.">
<figcaption><em>Zenpō kaiten</em> (前方回転)</figcaption>
</figure>

In _zenpō kaiten_ (前方回転) you bend forward from the defensive stance so that your body forms an arc running from the outstretched lead arm and the shoulder. Tuck your head. Then roll over the lead arm, shoulder, back, and hip, one after the other. Your legs stay close to the floor throughout the roll, and your head never touches it.

- two-handed (_ryōte_, 両手)
- one-handed (_katate_, 片手)
- without hands (_mute_, 無手)

## Kōhō kaiten – Backward Roll

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/koho-kaiten.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in four phases: from a crouch he sits back, rolls backward over the shoulder, and comes back to a crouch.">
<figcaption><em>Kōhō kaiten</em> (後方回転)</figcaption>
</figure>

For _kōhō kaiten_ (後方回転) you drop straight down from the defensive stance. Round your back and tuck your head. Your legs stay close to your body as you roll, and the roll goes sideways over the shoulder.

- two-handed (_ryōte_)
- one-handed (_katate_)
- without hands (_mute_)

## Sokuhō kaiten – Side Roll

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/sokuho-kaiten.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in three phases: from a crouch he rolls across his back with one leg swung high and comes up on one knee.">
<figcaption><em>Sokuhō kaiten</em> (側方回転)</figcaption>
</figure>

In _sokuhō kaiten_ (側方回転) you shift your center of gravity over one leg from the defensive stance and swing the other leg up in an arc in front of the supporting leg. Then you let your buttocks drop to the floor and lift the outstretched leg, so that your body rolls over your back.

- two-handed (_ryōte_)
- one-handed (_katate_)
- without hands (_mute_)

## Zenpō ukemi – Forward Fall

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/zenpo-ukemi.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in three phases: he stands upright, tips forward with a straight body, and lands flat on the floor on his forearms.">
<figcaption><em>Zenpō ukemi</em> (前方受身)</figcaption>
</figure>

Start practicing the forward fall, _zenpō ukemi_ (前方受身), on your knees. The forearms form a triangle, and palms and forearms catch the fall. Lift your chin so that your face doesn’t hit the floor. When you fall from a standing position, one leg stretches upward.

- on the knees, two-handed (_ryōte_)
- on the knees, one-handed (_katate_)
- standing, two-handed (_ryōte_)
- standing, one-handed (_katate_)

## Kōhō ukemi – Backward Fall

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/koho-ukemi.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in three phases: he stands upright, sinks into a deep squat, and then lies on his back with the arms on the floor at his sides and the legs raised.">
<figcaption><em>Kōhō ukemi</em> (後方受身)</figcaption>
</figure>

In the backward fall, _kōhō ukemi_ (後方受身), you slap the floor beside you with your arms and hands at an angle of 45°. One leg is bent with the sole of the foot on the floor. The other is bent sideways and inward, its foot landing first, flat on its outer edge. Press your chin to your chest so that the back of your head doesn’t hit the floor.

- on the knees, two-handed (_ryōte_)
- standing, two-handed (_ryōte_)

## Sokuhō ukemi – Side Fall

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/sokuho-ukemi.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in six phases: from a wide stance he drops low, falls to the side, rolls through, and comes back to standing via a kick.">
<figcaption><em>Sokuhō ukemi</em> (側方受身)</figcaption>
</figure>

In the side fall, _sokuhō ukemi_ (側方受身), you slap the floor with your palm and with your arm stretched out at 45° from your body. You don’t land on your back but on your side: hip, ribs, shoulder. Your head stays off the floor. Both legs point straight up together, about 45° from the floor.

- standing, to the left (_hidari katate_)
- standing, to the right (_migi katate_)

## Nagare – Flowing Water

_Nagare_ (流れ) means falling without a sound, soft and flowing like water. Your legs approach the floor flat first, your center of gravity sinks, and sometimes you roll straight on so that no sound is made.

- standing, backward (_tachi nagare_)
- standing, to the right (_migi yoko nagare_)
- standing, to the left (_hidari yoko nagare_)
- swirling backward (_kōhō tomoe nagare_)
- rolling backward (_kōhō kuruma nagare_)
- from _ichimonji no kamae_ (_jun nagare_)
- from _ichimonji no kamae_ (_gyaku nagare_)

## Shihō tenchi tobi – Leaping in Four Directions, to Heaven and Earth

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/shiho-tenchi-tobi.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in four phases: from a crouch he leaps straight up with raised arms, tucks his knees in the air, and lands in a crouch again.">
<figcaption><em>Shihō tenchi tobi</em> (四方天地飛)</figcaption>
</figure>

A ninja jumps lightly and loosely, so the jumps stay almost silent. In _shihō tenchi tobi_ (四方天地飛) you practice jumping flat and far to the left, right, front, and back. _Ten_ (天) means leaping high into the sky, _chi_ (地) means making yourself small and letting your body drop to the earth.

- forward (_mae_)
- backward (_kōhō_)
- to the right (_migi_)
- to the left (_hidari_)
- up (_ten_)
- down (_chi_)

<img src="/images/ukemi/sokuho-tobi.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in five phases: from a wide, low stance he leaps flat to the side and lands in the same stance.">
<figcaption><em>Sokuhō tobi</em></figcaption>
</figure>

## Ōten – Cartwheel

A cartwheel, _ōten_ (横転), lets you escape an attack from the side or break free of a joint lock.

- with one hand (_katate_)
- with two hands (_ryōte_)
- without hands (_mute_)

## Kiten – Handspring

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/kiten.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in six phases: from a crouch he springs over his hands into a handstand, goes over into a bridge, and comes back to his feet.">
<figcaption><em>Kiten</em></figcaption>
</figure>

For _kiten_ you jump from an upright stance with outstretched arms, forward, backward, or to the side, and downward, until your body weight is over your bent arms. The arms push the body up, and the bent legs snap out in the direction of the fall. The back is arched, the hips point the same way as the feet.

- two-handed forward (_mae ryōte_)
- one-handed forward (_mae katate_)
- two-handed backward (_kōhō ryōte_)
- one-handed backward (_kōhō katate_)
- two-handed sideways to the right or left (_migi/hidari ryōte_)
- one-handed sideways to the right or left (_migi/hidari katate_)

<img src="/images/ukemi/sokuho-kiten.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in five phases: from a wide stance he turns sideways over his hands into a straddled handstand and lands in a wide stance again.">
<figcaption><em>Sokuhō kiten</em></figcaption>
</figure>

## Kūten – Air Turn

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/kuten.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in four phases: he takes off with one arm raised, turns tucked in the air, and lands in a crouch.">
<figcaption><em>Kūten</em> (空転)</figcaption>
</figure>

Once you have mastered the handspring, you can move on to the air turn, _kūten_ (空転). You spring up sharply, forward or backward, pull your legs to your body, and turn around your own axis in the air.

- forward (_mae_)
- backward (_kōhō_)

## Kūhi – Air Leap

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/kuhi.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in four phases: he runs up, takes off, flies horizontally with his arms stretched forward, and rolls up curled on the floor.">
<figcaption><em>Kūhi</em> (空飛)</figcaption>
</figure>

The air leap, _kūhi_ (空飛), is for throwing yourself at an opponent or clearing an obstacle. You land either with a roll or by catching yourself on your hands and forearms.

## Shōten no jutsu – The Art of Climbing to Heaven

<figure>
<img src="/images/ukemi/shoten-no-jutsu.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in four phases: he runs up, plants a foot on a steeply angled wooden board, runs up it, and grips the top edge.">
<figcaption><em>Shōten no jutsu</em> (昇天の術)</figcaption>
</figure>

With _shōten no jutsu_ (昇天の術) you run up a wall, a fence, a tree, or a person. You have to be fast enough. With enough momentum you jump at the obstacle, push yourself up with your legs, and leap over it.

To practice, use a board that you set up at a steeper and steeper angle.

- jumping down from above (_tobi ori_, 飛び降り)
- running up vertical surfaces (_shōten_)
