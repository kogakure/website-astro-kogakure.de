Over water, under water, across swampland: with these tools the ninja moved quickly and left armored samurai behind with ease.

## Hasami Bune

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/hasami-bune.webp" alt="A shallow open box of dark wooden boards, its corners lashed together with loops of rope.">
<figcaption><em>Hasami bune</em></figcaption>
</figure>

A collapsible boat the ninja used to carry their equipment across the water. The <cite>Bansenshūkai</cite> describes such a folding boat as the _hasamibako bune_ (挟箱船): its boards were joined by hinges, and folded up it fit into a carrying chest. Another name is _tatami bune_.

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/tatami-bune.webp" alt="A small open wooden punt of unfolded boards, its sides and end boards slanting outward, iron hinges along the floor.">
<figcaption><em>Tatami bune</em></figcaption>
</figure>

## Kama Ikada

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/kama-ikada.webp" alt="A narrow raft of long, tightly bundled stalks, tied with cord in several places.">
<figcaption><em>Kama ikada</em></figcaption>
</figure>

A small one-person raft of wood or straw that could cover long distances across rivers or lakes.

## Kataga Hasami Bune

A light, narrow boat.

## Kyobako Fune

A wooden box covered with leather on the outside.

## Mizugumo

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/mizugumo.webp" alt="Four padded sacks tied with cord in a circle around a dark central cushion.">
<figcaption><em>Mizugumo</em> (水蜘蛛)</figcaption>
</figure>

The _mizugumo_ (水蜘蛛) is a set of leather sacks filled with air. Sitting on the middle cushion, legs in the water, a ninja crossed open water and paddled forward with the fold-out _shinobi kai_.

## Mizugumo in the Swamp

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/mizugumo-ring.webp" alt="A flat wooden ring of four curved plank segments, a small rectangular footboard in the round opening, lashed to the ring with crossed hemp cords.">
<figcaption><em>Mizugumo</em> (水蜘蛛)</figcaption>
</figure>

This _mizugumo_ was most likely used for walking across swampland: wooden boards tied to the feet kept the wearer from sinking. For snow, the ninja probably used a device that must have been very similar to modern snowshoes.

## Mizukaki

_Mizukaki_ (水掻き) are swim fins.

## Mizu Tsutsu

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/mizu-tsutsu.webp" alt="A plain bamboo tube, open at both ends, with its node ridges showing.">
<figcaption><em>Mizu tsutsu</em></figcaption>
</figure>

To stay underwater for a long time, a ninja breathed through a reed or a wooden tube.

## Shinobi Bune

The _shinobi bune_ (忍び舟) is a light, narrow boat.

## Shinobi Kai

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/shinobi-kai.webp" alt="A bamboo shaft with a fan of thin slats at one end and a pull cord with a knotted stopper at the other.">
<figcaption><em>Shinobi kai</em></figcaption>
</figure>

A hollow bamboo staff held a few iron parts that quickly turned into a paddle.

## Taru Ikada

Another name for the _uki daru_.

## Tsugi Bune

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/tsugi-bune.webp" alt="A long, narrow boat made of four box-shaped wooden sections joined end to end, the front section tapering toward the bow.">
<figcaption><em>Tsugi bune</em></figcaption>
</figure>

According to tradition, in a larger ninja team each member carried one part of the boat. At the water’s edge, they quickly put the parts together.

## Uki Daru

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/uki-daru.webp" alt="Two small wooden barrels wrapped and joined with rope, each with a strapped footboard inside.">
<figcaption><em>Uki daru</em></figcaption>
</figure>

One leg in each of the two floating barrels, a pole in hand to steady and push off: according to ninja books, this way a ninja could cover long distances across the water.
