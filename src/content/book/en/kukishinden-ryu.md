## The School of the Nine Demon Gods

No school of the Bujinkan has as many connections and relatives among the other _ryūha_ (流派) as the **Kukishinden-Ryū** (九鬼神伝流) _happō hikenjutsu_ (八法秘剣術). It descends from the Kukishin-Ryū, which has other _sōke_ (宗家) as well, for example in the Hontai-Yōshin-Ryū.

If the story is true, the Kukishinden-Ryū is a branch school of the Kukishin-Ryū. Takamatsu was allowed to found it in gratitude for reconstructing the school’s _densho_ (伝書). He had originally been declared _sōke_ of the Kukishin-Ryū. But because he was considered missing during his travels in China, he had to give up the title to Iwami Nangaku.

<img src="/images/article/kukishinden-ryu-02-reconstructing-densho.webp" alt="A young man kneels in lamplight among unrolled, damaged scrolls, piecing together torn sheets with drawings of fighting techniques.">
<figcaption>Reconstructing the <em>densho</em></figcaption>

Aikidō (合気道) is also said to descend from the Kukishin-Ryū: Ueshiba Morihei is said to have learned his staff techniques from the _sōke_ of the Kukishin-Ryū. And the founder of jūdō (柔道), Kanō Jigorō, is said to have learned techniques from Takamatsu Sensei.
