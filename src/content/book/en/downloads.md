Here you can download a font based on a secret script of the Iga ninja and 3D models of ninja weapons and tools.

## Iga Ninja Font

<figure>
<img src="/images/old/downloads/ingo.jpg" alt="The characters of the Iga ninja cipher in five rows against a misty conifer forest, each with its katakana syllable below.">
<figcaption>Iga ninja font</figcaption>
</figure>

Many years ago, the Iga Ninja Museum published an image of an Iga ninja cipher on its website. I turned it into a font.

- [View the Font on GitHub](https://github.com/kogakure/font-ingo)
- [Download the Font](/downloads/iga-ninja-font.zip)
