## Fitness and Mobility

In ninjutsu, strength comes from flexibility. That idea drives **_jūnantaisō_** (柔軟体操), the conditioning training of the ninja. It improves suppleness, speed, and reactions so that you can apply the techniques of _taijutsu_ (体術) effectively. Muscles and tendons regain their natural elasticity. Combined with a proper diet, _jūnantaisō_ keeps the body healthy into old age.

Muscle training falls into two areas. Weak muscles are strengthened with fast repetitions. This kind of training is marked by a rapid alternation of contraction and relaxation, and it can leave you feeling physically exhausted.

The second area loosens stiff, immobile muscles through stretching. This kind of training is also known from Indian yoga. Each position is held for a few seconds while the muscles stay relaxed.

_Jūnantaisō_ is not a simple warm-up before combat practice. It lays a vital, healthy foundation on which the body learns again to work in harmony with all its parts.

The figures below show a few examples. Beyond these there are countless other exercises for flexibility, strength, and endurance: pull-ups, sit-ups, various push-ups, and stretches for every part of the body.
