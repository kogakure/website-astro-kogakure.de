## Bōenkyō

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/boenkyo.webp" alt="A brass draw-tube telescope with a lacquered wooden barrel and turned end caps.">
<figcaption><em>Bōenkyō</em> (望遠鏡)</figcaption>
</figure>

With a kind of telescope, the _bōenkyō_ (望遠鏡), the ninja watched the enemy army from a safe distance. Sometimes it was disguised as a dagger, a _tantō_ (短刀).

## Hito Washi

The ninja are said to have known a flying device, the _hito washi_ or _yamidoko_, that let them glide through the air. With a mission done, they supposedly jumped off a fortress wall with it and flew away.

## Inrō

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/inro.webp" alt="A small stacked lacquer case with several compartments, hanging on a silk cord with a bead and a carved toggle.">
<figcaption><em>Inrō</em> (印籠)</figcaption>
</figure>

In the medicine case _inrō_ (印籠), the ninja carried every remedy a mission might call for: medicine for pain, cuts, poisoning, and other ailments. The ninja’s poisons were kept in it as well.

## Kagami

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/kagami.webp" alt="A round Japanese bronze hand mirror with a short handle and a highly polished face catching the light.">
<figcaption><em>Kagami</em> (鏡)</figcaption>
</figure>

The bronze mirror _kagami_ (鏡) flashed signals to comrades or allies. It could also blind the enemy.

## Kishaku

The floating compass _kishaku_ (耆著) goes back to a Chinese model, the <q>south-pointing fish</q>. A magnetized piece of iron, often shaped like a fish or a boat, floated in a bowl of water. The <cite>Bansenshūkai</cite> explains how to make one: a piece of thin iron is heated red-hot and quenched in water. That way a ninja could find directions even without a ready-made compass.

## Onibi

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/onibi.webp" alt="A carved demon mask lacquered red and black, with bulging eyes, short horns, and bared teeth.">
<figcaption><em>Onibi</em></figcaption>
</figure>

With gruesome wooden masks, the _onibi_, the ninja struck terror into pursuers. That stopped the chase, or at least broke their fighting spirit.

## Suikatsugan

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/kishi-yaku.webp" alt="Small dark herbal pills in a folded paper twist beside a small lacquer box.">
<figcaption><em>Suikatsugan</em> (水渇丸)</figcaption>
</figure>

Against thirst, the ninja carried the _suikatsugan_ (水渇丸), a pill made from pickled-plum flesh, sugar, and dwarf lilyturf root. The <cite>Bansenshūkai</cite> records the recipe. The pill kept the mouth and throat moist, so that on long journeys a ninja could go longer without water.

## Yamidoko

_Yamidoko_ is another name for the _hito washi_.
