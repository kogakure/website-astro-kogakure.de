Ninja used the _chikairi_ (近入, <q>close entry</q>) tactics at the start of a confrontation or while it was under way. They consist mainly of sabotage and infiltration techniques.

## Fukuro Gaeshi Zen Jutsu

If a ninja managed to intercept a message between the enemy lord and his allies or relatives, the ninja forged it and sent it on its way. With luck, the ninja could intercept and fully control the correspondence in both directions. In this way the ninja sowed distrust and hostility between allies.

## Geinyū Jutsu

In this tactic, the ninja entered enemy territory to spread confusion, fear, and terror. The ninja anticipated the enemy’s troop movements and countered them at once, or spread false rumors, stirred the discontented to revolt and unrest, set fires at strategically important points, and sowed distrust among the soldiers. Horses, weapons, and food were stolen or made useless. When the enemy was about to march into a village, the ninja ran ahead and threw the inhabitants into panic.

## Katagatae Jutsu

When the enemy left camp, the ninja seized the chance and crept inside. There the ninja sabotaged important facilities, threw the remaining guards into panic, and opened the gates for their own troops.

## Ryōhan Jutsu

Ninja could also kidnap key figures to force through their terms. They blackmailed the victims’ followers: either serve the ninja, or the kidnapping victim would be executed.

## Ryakuhon Jutsu

Here the ninja crept into the enemy camp and posed as an ally. This only worked if the ninja was already known there and knew the area thoroughly.

## Suigetsu Jutsu

In this tactic (<q>moon in the water</q>), the ninja staged a diversion. A squad of ninja or part of the regular army launched a mock attack on one point of the fortress. While every defender rushed to the threatened spot, the ninja climbed the wall where no one was watching.

## Yoja Jutsu

In this tactic, the ninja took on the appearance of someone defenseless and harmless: a beggar, a lame man, a blind, deaf, or deaf-mute person, seeking shelter inside the castle. The ninja could also get into the castle together with traveling entertainers or merchants.
