After a mission, the ninja had to get away. For this they relied on **_gotonpō_** (五遁法), the five methods of escape. Each one uses one of the five elements and rests on the Daoist principle of _gogyō_ (五行): with water (_suiton_, 水遁), wood (_mokuton_, 木遁), fire (_katon_, 火遁), earth (_doton_, 土遁), and metal (_kinton_, 金遁), the ninja prepared and carried out the escape.

## Suiton – Escape with Water

- Using still and flowing water to escape
- Swimming and diving techniques
- Boats and water tools
- Silent swimming
- Swimming without making waves
- Hiding behind artificial reed islands (_kitsune gakure no jutsu_, <q>the art of hiding like a fox</q>)
- Staying underwater with a breathing tube or air bags
- Knowing how water affects a battle
- Diverting rivers
- Undermining walls
- Flooding the land
- Navigating on water

## Mokuton – Escape with Wood

- Using wood and plants to escape
- Climbing trees and poles (_tanuki gakure no jutsu_, <q>the art of hiding like a raccoon dog</q>)
- Hiding in treetops and bushes
- Natural camouflage
- Making poisons and medicines
- Building siege engines
- Knowledge of building structure and construction
- Climbing with ropes and ladders

## Katon – Escape with Fire

- Using fire
- Firearms, grenades, mines, cannons, and explosives
- Exploiting light, shadow, and sound
- Exploiting dawn and dusk, fog, rain, and smoke
- Sneaking through the shadows
- Poison gas and sleeping gas
- Arson
- Knowing how fire works in battle
- Setting fields and forests on fire
- How wind drives fire
- Fire arrows and incendiary projectiles
- Smoking out

## Doton – Escape with Earth

- Using earth, rocks, stones, and walls to escape
- Hiding in holes, caves, and hollows, and behind rocks
- Burying oneself in sand and earth
- Climbing rocks and walls
- Walking and running techniques for roofs, narrow passages, and slippery ground
- Knowledge of terrain
- Knowledge of topography
- Navigating on land
- Knowledge of vehicles

## Kinton – Escape with Metal

- Using metal to escape
- Weapons such as _shuriken_ (手裏剣) and _tetsubishi_ (鉄菱)
- Tools such as saws, drills, and burglary tools
- Climbing gear such as _shukō_ (手鉤), _ashi kō_, and _kagi nawa_ (鈎縄)
- Mirrors for dazzling or signaling

## How the Elements Combine

The five elements are hard to separate, and usually several come into play at once. A ninja climbing rocks (_doton_) used metal climbing tools (_kinton_) to do it. A ninja hiding in a hole in the ground (_doton_) or in water (_suiton_) often added plants (_mokuton_) as camouflage. The categories can be extended at will. Even modern technology such as GPS fits into one of them.
