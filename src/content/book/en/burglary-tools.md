The ninja are said to have broken into any fortress and out of any prison. For this they had a large collection of tools for sawing, drilling, and digging.

## Kasugai

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/kasugai.webp" alt="A U-shaped forged iron staple with both ends sharpened to a point.">
<figcaption><em>Kasugai</em> (鎹)</figcaption>
</figure>

Driven into the floor and the door, the _kasugai_ (鎹) kept a door from opening. That blocked the enemy’s way and kept the ninja’s back free.

## Kiri

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/kiri.webp" alt="A slim iron blade with a row of fine saw teeth along one edge and a short iron handle.">
<figcaption><em>Kiri</em> (切小刀)</figcaption>
</figure>

The _kiri_ (切小刀) is a slim, pointed saw for cutting open locks.

## Kunai

<figure>
<img src="/images/weapons/kunai.webp" alt="A leaf-shaped, roughly forged iron blade with a thick spine and a ring at the end of the handle.">
<figcaption><em>Kunai</em> (苦無)</figcaption>
</figure>

The _kunai_ (苦無) was used for digging and prying.

## Kunashi

A collapsible spade.

## Shikoro

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/shikoro.webp" alt="A narrow, thin saw with fine teeth and a straight wooden handle.">
<figcaption><em>Shikoro</em></figcaption>
</figure>

With this thin saw the ninja got through wooden walls and wooden doors.

## Tobikunai

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/tobikunai.webp" alt="A long iron fork with two slender tines and a round wooden handle, a small sickle blade hanging from a cord at the fork.">
<figcaption><em>Tobikunai</em></figcaption>
</figure>

The _tobikunai_ could open barred gates.

## Tsubo Giri

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/tsubo-giri.webp" alt="Several iron tools laid side by side: two pointed awls with wooden handles, two forks with crescent-shaped tines, and a half-moon blade.">
<figcaption><em>Tsubo giri</em></figcaption>
</figure>

This drill cut larger holes in wooden walls and wooden doors.

## Yajiri

<figure>
<img src="/images/tools/yajiri.webp" alt="A long, slim, knife-shaped saw with a wooden handle, teeth along both edges of the tapering blade.">
<figcaption><em>Yajiri</em></figcaption>
</figure>

For thicker pieces of wood or branches, the ninja used a saw with a sturdier blade.
