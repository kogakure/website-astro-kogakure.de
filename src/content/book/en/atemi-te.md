## Pressing and Striking the Nerve Points

About 250 spots on the human body are sensitive to pressure, blows, and thrusts. Hitting them on purpose is **_atemite_**. The effects vary widely. Some points cause only harmless pain that makes the opponent give up. Sixty-nine count as critical, because a hit can cause unconsciousness, paralysis, or death. These are the places where nerve centers, vital organs, or dense clusters of blood vessels lie.

<aside class="banner">

**Warning**

In training, **never** strike the points with full force. Only touch them lightly or press with slowly increasing pressure to check whether you have found the point.

</aside>

A ninja knows the points of the body precisely, both to protect themselves better and to defend themselves effectively.

For _atemi_ (当身) to work, the strike must be mastered perfectly and placed with great precision, on the smallest possible area. If you don’t want to kill your opponent outright, you have to judge their constitution and measure the force of the blow accordingly.

To protect yourself, strong chest and abdominal muscles are essential.
