## Development of a Natural Consciousness

The ninja wanted to become <q>thoroughly natural beings</q>. Their goal was _kiai_ (気合) consciousness. To reach it, you have to experience the workings of the cosmic laws, the universal consciousness, directly in yourself. You observe nature without prejudice and understand your own connection to all things. This leads to self-knowledge.

For this, the ninja used two systems of looking at nature: the _godai_ (五大) and the _gogyō_ (五行).

## Five Elemental Manifestations

The _godai_ comes from _mikkyō_ (密教). It teaches that everything physical comes from one source and can be assigned to one of five basic manifestations:

- **_Kū_ (空, void):** the origin of subatomic energy, nothingness, the beginning of all things
- **_Fū_ (風, wind):** the gaseous elements
- **_Ka_ (火, fire):** the energy-releasing elements
- **_Sui_ (水, water):** the liquid elements
- **_Chi_ (地, earth):** the solid elements

## The Creation of the Universe

The creation of the universe shows how the principle is meant. In the void (_kū_), polarity and electromagnetic charges arose. From them formed atoms and electrons, and with them the various gases (_fū_). When these gases reacted with one another, they did so on the energy-releasing plane (_ka_). The molecules that resulted became vapor (_sui_). From this, solid matter finally emerged (_chi_).

This is a greatly simplified picture of creation, but it helps understanding. The human being can be seen the same way. Bones, teeth, muscles, and tendons are solid matter, the bodily fluids liquid matter. Body heat and combustion correspond to fire, breathing to air. The void shows itself in the ability to speak and communicate.

## Five Levels of Consciousness

Human consciousness, too, shifts back and forth between these levels. Each level has a color, a seat in the body, and an example from nature:

| Level | Color | Seat | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earth | Red | Base of the spine | Rocks |
| Water | Orange | _hara_ (腹), the lower abdominal cavity | Plants |
| Fire | Yellow | Solar plexus, at the lower tip of the breastbone | Wild animals |
| Air | Green | Center of the chest | – |
| Void | Blue | Throat | – |

### Earth

At the level of earth, you feel your own stability. You resist change and movement and wish things would stay as they are. Rocks embody this level: they don’t move, don’t grow, and don’t change unless something acts on them.

### Water

At the level of water, you are aware of your feelings and bodily fluids. Adaptation and reaction define this level. You react to everything that happens and tend toward melancholy. Plants are the best example: they grow and adapt to their environment, but they cannot rule it.

### Fire

At the level of fire, you experience your own dynamic, expansive nature. You feel joy and warmth, want to subdue your surroundings, and know your mental and physical potential. Wild animals stand for this level. They can remember and think, and they seek pleasure and joy.

### Air

At the level of air, you feel wisdom and love. You recognize your own intellect and goodwill and meet others with compassion and understanding.

### Void

The level of the void brings creativity and the ability to move from any level to any other.

## Five Ways of Fighting

Each level has its own fighter.

The **earth** fighter is unshakable. They stand firm and withstand every attack. Fighting them is like fighting a boulder.

The **water** fighter is always on the move, dodging and making sudden, unexpected moves. Fighting them is like fighting the waves: they pull back and strike again.

The **fire** fighter fights with fierce determination, hard and violent. They are like a brush fire – the harder you beat at the flames, the stronger they burn.

The **air** fighter fights defensively. They protect themselves but do little harm to the opponent. They intercept attacks and redirect energy. Fighting them is like fighting the wind: they dance around you, and you can never catch hold of them.

At the level of the **void**, there is no fight. Thoughts, words, and deeds create a situation in which a fight never begins.

## The Elements in Daily Life

Each level can show itself positively or negatively. With earth, the positive side is stability, the negative self-destructive stubbornness. With water, flexibility stands against sentimentality; with fire, aggressive vitality against overwhelming fear. With air, the positive side can appear as wisdom and love, the negative as a spirituality that hides the truth.

How someone deals with fear, joy, or boredom, which movies they love, what drives them, how they argue, or what advice they give: all of it can be assigned to one of the elemental levels. Most people move between levels; some have settled on one. There is no <q>good</q> or <q>bad</q> here. Everything is right and fitting, because everything is part of the universe. In this way you can understand what drives a person and which problems torment them.
