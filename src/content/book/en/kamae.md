## Expression of Inner Attitude

The fighting stances of _taijutsu_ (体術) tell the body how to move, but that says little about them. They are best understood as an expression of inner attitude. In combat as in life, what matters is that body and mind are one. Performed correctly, the **_kamae_** (構え) reflect the heart of the ninja.

In other martial arts, every fighter takes the same position, whether or not it suits their body. That is not the point of _kamae_, and neither is imitating animals. The fighter should move naturally, with body, mind, and spirit as one: _shingitai_ (心技体).

## Flowing, Not Static

A _kamae_ is not a photograph. You hold it as long as it serves you and leave it the moment the situation changes; one stance flows into the next. Say your opponent kicks low. From _shizen no kamae_ (自然の構え) you fall back into _ichimonji no kamae_ (一文字の構え), lift the threatened foot (_hichō no kamae_, 飛鳥の構え), kick the attacker’s knee, and land in _jūmonji no kamae_ (十文字の構え). From there you shift into _kōsei no kamae_ (攻勢の構え) and strike at the opponent’s back. Five movements in two to three seconds. No stance is held a moment longer than necessary, unlike other martial arts, where fighters remain in a stance.

## Natural Movement

As students progress, the _kamae_ matter less and less as specific positions. They become part of the fighter’s natural movement. Once you have absorbed the basics, the most important fighting stance becomes the non-fighting stance.

There are a great many fighting stances. Some differ from one Ryū to another or go by other names there. Each can also be taken at different heights, kneeling for example, or aimed at different heights.

Some of these stances are explained and shown below.

## Observing Stances

The observing stances are the basic stances. They are the standing and sitting postures you are usually in when an attack comes. From them you also bow and show respect to your opponent. They were developed in the East, where, unlike in the West, there were no chairs. That is why they are natural sitting and standing positions.

### Gasshō no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/gassho-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing stands upright, feet shoulder-width apart, palms pressed together in front of the chest with the fingers pointing up.">
<figcaption><em>Gasshō no kamae</em> (合掌の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

_Gasshō no kamae_ (合掌の構え) is the standing salute. Both feet stand firmly on the ground, the hands are placed together in front of the torso, and the elbows are slightly raised. The gaze runs over the fingertips straight to the opponent.

### Fudōza no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/fudoza-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing sits on the floor with folded legs, back straight, hands resting on the knees.">
<figcaption><em>Fudōza no kamae</em> (不動座の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

_Fudōza no kamae_ (不動座の構え) is the traditional Japanese sitting posture. The left leg is folded under the body, and you sit on the ankle of the left foot. The right leg is drawn up in front of the body, the sole of the right foot against the knee. The back is straight, the spine in its natural position. The gaze is straight ahead.

### Seiza no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/seiza-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing kneels upright, sitting on the heels, hands flat on the thighs.">
<figcaption><em>Seiza no kamae</em> (正座の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

_Seiza no kamae_ (正座の構え) is the traditional Japanese kneeling posture. The legs are folded under the body, and you sit on the soles of your feet. The upper body is upright, the knees slightly apart, the hands resting on the thighs. To show respect, you bend the upper body forward and place your palms on the floor in front of you.

## Defending Stances

The defending stances come into play when you have to block or evade an attack before you can counter. The body slips or glides inward or outward, away from the attack. From this position you block or attack.

### Ichi no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/ichi-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing stands side-on, weight on the rear leg, the lead arm extended straight forward, the rear hand at the hip.">
<figcaption><em>Ichi no kamae</em> (一の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

For _ichi no kamae_ (一の構え) you step one leg forward from _shizen_. Most of your weight stays on the back leg. The body is turned at an angle away from the enemy. The front hand points toward the enemy, the rear hand stays at the belt as a fist. From this position you move into _ichimonji no kamae_.

### Ichimonji no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/ichimonji-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in a deep side-on stance, weight back, the lead hand open and extended toward the opponent at shoulder height, the rear hand open in front of the lead shoulder.">
<figcaption><em>Ichimonji no kamae</em> (一文字の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

In _ichimonji no kamae_ the body pulls back, away from the attack. The rear leg keeps it upright. The shoulders are relaxed and the hips turned so that the vital points stay hidden. The open hands protect the face and body or catch the opponent’s attack.

### Doko no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/doko-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing in a deep side-on stance, weight back, the rear fist raised beside the head, the lead hand open and extended forward.">
<figcaption><em>Doko no kamae</em> (怒虎の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

In _doko no kamae_ (怒虎の構え), too, the body pulls back, away from the attack. The rear leg keeps it upright, and the front leg is ready to push it back even further if needed. The front hand blocks or catches the opponent’s blows. The rear hand is held close to the ear, ready for the counterstrike.

### Hichō no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/hicho-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing balances on one bent leg, the other foot drawn up against the supporting knee, the lead hand open and extended forward.">
<figcaption><em>Hichō no kamae</em> (飛鳥の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

_Hichō no kamae_ evades an attack while the raised front leg waits to strike the opponent. The supporting leg is slightly bent for balance. The arms are held ready to grab, block, or strike.

## Receiving Stances

The receiving stances are used when you meet an attack with techniques that harmonize with the opponent’s force or go along with it. The body turns or moves aside and blends with the enemy’s movement. Defenses work the same way.

### Shizen no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/shizen-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing stands naturally upright, feet shoulder-width apart, arms hanging relaxed at the sides.">
<figcaption><em>Shizen no kamae</em> (自然の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

_Shizen no kamae_ is the natural standing position of the human body. The mind is alert, the body relaxed and ready to move and react to the opponent. The weight rests on both feet, the shoulders are loose, and the arms hang naturally at the sides. Most attacks will find you in this stance, because it is how people naturally stand and walk. That makes it the most important stance, and it deserves thorough training.

### Hanmi no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/hanmi-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing stands half turned away, feet at an angle to each other, arms hanging relaxed, the lead hand slightly forward.">
<figcaption><em>Hanmi no kamae</em> (半身の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

For _hanmi no kamae_ (半身の構え) you simply take a small step forward from _shizen no kamae_. The body turns away from the opponent as you do.

### Goku no kamae

In _goku no kamae_ you turn the body 45° away from the opponent, starting from _shizen no kamae_. The hands hang naturally as in _shizen_, the front hand open, the rear hand in _fudō ken_ (不動拳).

### Hira ichimonji no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/hira-ichimonji-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing faces the viewer in a wide stance with bent knees, both arms stretched straight out to the sides at shoulder height.">
<figcaption><em>Hira ichimonji no kamae</em></figcaption>
</figure>

_Hira ichimonji no kamae_ is a balanced standing position from which the body can evade. The weight is spread evenly over both feet, and both arms are stretched out to the sides. They can sweep, grab, or catch blows.

### Hōko no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/hoko-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing stands with bent knees, both arms raised high above the head and slightly rounded, palms facing forward.">
<figcaption><em>Hōko no kamae</em></figcaption>
</figure>

_Hōko no kamae_ is another stance from which the body can respond to an attack. The weight rests on both feet, and the arms are held slightly bent above the shoulders. They can catch, redirect, or strike.

## Attacking Stances

The attacking stances take you from defense to attack. They are only partly defensive and mostly offensive. Kicks, punches, and throws are launched from them.

### Jūmonji no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/jumonji-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing stands with bent knees, fists crossed at the wrists in front of the chest, elbows out.">
<figcaption><em>Jūmonji no kamae</em> (十文字の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

_Jūmonji no kamae_ is easy to recognize by the crossed wrists. This position of the hands protects the body during an attack: the elbows cover the ribs, and the fists guard the neck and face before they strike. The weight rests slightly more on the front foot, and the rear foot drives the body forward.

### Kōsei no kamae

<figure>
<img src="/images/kamae/kosei-no-kamae.webp" alt="A masked ninja in dark clothing stands with the weight back, one hand raised open high above the head, the other a fist in front of the belly.">
<figcaption><em>Kōsei no kamae</em> (攻勢の構え)</figcaption>
</figure>

_Kōsei no kamae_ is used to attack, but it also lets you catch incoming blows. The front hand grabs, redirects, or draws the opponent’s attention. The rear hand carries out the attack.
