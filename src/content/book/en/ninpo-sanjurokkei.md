## The 36 Military Strategies

A ninja had to learn twice eighteen fields. First, he needed a good grounding in the 18 categories of martial arts, the _bugei jūhappan_ (武芸十八般), and tried to become a specialist in them. On top of that came 18 categories of secret techniques, the _ninja jūhakkei_.

The number 36 is considered special because it can be broken down into 3 and 6. Added together, 3 and 6 make 9; multiplied, they make 18. Split 18 into 1 and 8, and the sum is 9 again. In the martial arts, especially the Chinese ones, 9 carries a special meaning. It is said to stand for perfection, because 10 begins a new, two-digit range of numbers.

The _sanjūrokkei_ were not fixed. Fields were often dropped and others added. Nor did a ninja limit himself to the _ninpō sanjūrokkei_; he always tried to perfect himself in as many fields as possible.

### The Three Pillars

Three of the 36 fields carry special weight: _taijutsu_ (体術), unarmed combat; _bikenjutsu_, sword fighting; and _bōjutsu_ (棒術), stick fighting. They are also called the <q>three pillars</q> of _ninpō_ (忍法).

Just as important is _seishin teki kyōyō_ (精神的教養), spiritual development. Without the spiritual and philosophical aspect, a martial art degenerates into an empty shell with no value.

## Bugei Jūhappan

1. _Jūjutsu_ (柔術), _kenpō_ (拳法): unarmed defense
2. _Kenjutsu_ (剣術), _gekkenjutsu_: sword
3. _Iai jutsu_ (居合術), _battōjutsu_: sword drawing
4. _Kusari fundōjutsu_: chains
5. _Kusarigamajutsu_: chain and sickle
6. _Bōjutsu_: staff
7. _Sōjutsu_: spear
8. _Naginatajutsu_: sword lance
9. _Suiren_ (水練): swimming
10. _Bajutsu_ (馬術): riding
11. _Kishajutsu_: mounted archery
12. _Jōjutsu_: walking stick
13. _Kyūjutsu_ (弓術): archery
14. _Yoroi kumiuchi_: fighting in armor
15. _Hōjō jutsu_ (捕縄術), _hobakujutsu_: binding
16. _Juttejutsu_: iron staff
17. _Hojutsu_: illusions
18. _Nage kenjutsu_, _shurikenjutsu_: throwing blades

## Ninja Jūhakkei

1. _Seishin teki kyōyō_: spiritual development
2. _Taijutsu_: unarmed combat, with _jū tai jutsu_ (柔体術), _daken taijutsu_ (打拳体術), _koppō jutsu_ (骨法術), and _kosshijutsu_
3. _Bikenjutsu_: sword, with _tantōjutsu_ (短刀術), _kodachijutsu_, _ōdachijutsu_, _ninja tōjutsu_, _ittōjutsu_, and _nitōjutsu_
4. _Sōjutsu_: spear
5. _Naginatajutsu_: sword lance
6. _Bisentōjutsu_: halberd
7. _Kusarigamajutsu_, _kyoketsu shōge_ (距跋渉毛): sickle and chain, rope
8. _Roku shaku bōjutsu_, _bōjutsu_: staff
9. _San shaku bō jutsu_, _jōjutsu_: walking stick
10. _Shurikenjutsu_, _senbannagejutsu_: throwing blades
11. _Kishajutsu_: mounted archery
12. _Inton jutsu_ (隠遁術): hiding, with _omote_ and _ura goton_ and _tenchijin santon_
13. _Hokō jutsu_ (歩行術): walking techniques
14. _Hensō jutsu_ (変装術): disguise, with _shichi hō de sanpo gata_
15. _Kakushi bukijutsu_: secret weapons such as _shukō_ (手鉤), _sokko_, _neko te_ (猫手), and others
16. _Ninyakujutsu_: medicine, drugs
17. _Gunryaku heihō_: strategies
18. _Tenmon_ (天文), _chimon_ (地文): strategies of heaven and earth
