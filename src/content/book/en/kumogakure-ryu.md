## The School of the Hidden Cloud

The _taijutsu_ (体術) of **Kumogakure-Ryū** (雲隠流) _ninpō_ (忍法) is very similar to that of the Togakure-Ryū (戸隠流). The school was probably founded by the Toda family.

The ninja of the Kumogakure-Ryū went into battle in armored clothing. Some of them wore a demon mask.

<figure>
<img src="/images/article/kumogakure-ryu-01-into-battle-in-demon-mask.webp" alt="A warrior in light, dark armor and a horned red demon mask stands on a foggy battlefield holding a long spear with a curved blade, banners and more warriors behind him.">
<figcaption>Into battle in a demon mask</figcaption>
</figure>

One of the school’s special weapons is the _kama yari_ (鎌槍), a hook spear. Against swordsmen, the hooks caught the blows. The same hooks were also used to kill opponents from above. According to the school’s tradition, the _densho_ (伝書) of the Kumogakure-Ryū mention a ninja named Sarutobi Sasuke who used the hook spear to swing from tree to tree.

<figure>
<img src="/images/article/kumogakure-ryu-02-tree-to-tree-with-kama-yari.webp" alt="A shinobi in dark clothing swings across a gorge from one tree to the next, hanging from his hook spear, its curved blade hooked over a branch.">
<figcaption>From tree to tree with the <em>kama yari</em></figcaption>
</figure>

Another specialty is survival training, along with the art of lighting a fire in any situation.

<figure>
<img src="/images/article/kumogakure-ryu-03-making-fire-in-any-weather.webp" alt="In pouring rain in the mountains, a ninja crouches under a rock overhang and blows into a bundle of tinder where a small flame glows.">
<figcaption>Making fire in any weather</figcaption>
</figure>

There are probably many techniques built on small steps, because women at the time wore the traditional kimono.
