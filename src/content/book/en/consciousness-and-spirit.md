## The Mind as a Bridge to Consciousness

A stone vibrates slowly, a thought quickly – or so this teaching holds: the universe consists of vibrations of different wavelengths. Solid matter has a very low rate of vibration, followed by sound, heat, electricity, light, and finally thought. Within each phenomenon, too, the wavelength determines how it is perceived: in light it sets the hue, in sound the pitch.

What arrives, however, depends on the receiver, and different beings take it in differently. Some animals hear ultrasound or see ultraviolet light.

The mind is the bridge between consciousness and body. It interprets the impressions the organism takes in.

The ninja of the Middle Ages knew nothing of wavelengths or vibration rates. But they were certain that everything in this universe has its place somewhere. It followed that no action is independent of another. Even phenomena that seem entirely unrelated at first glance are connected in some way.

The ninja sought to observe and influence their surroundings by tuning their thought waves to the same vibration as their environment.

## Enhance Your Senses

Before ninja reawaken their senses, they first become aware of how far they have dulled them in the past. In martial arts training, you first learn natural movement. In the same way, the natural perception of the senses is reawakened, one sense at a time.

For a while, the ninja pays close attention to every smell, strong ones as well as the faint nuances nobody usually notices. Even bad smells they simply perceive, without judging them.

For the sense of taste, the ninja attends only to taste for a time, chewing longer and more intently, or broadening their diet.

To awaken the eyes, the ninja pays particularly close attention to every visual impression. They concentrate on the shades of color in the sky at sunset and experiment with the focus of their eyes, with distances and shapes.

The ninja awakens the sense of touch by concentrating on what touches the body: tension, warmth and cold, surfaces. During this time, they touch a great many objects and run their fingers over the surfaces with eyes closed.

For hearing, the ninja pays attention to sounds and music. Not only to loud noise such as street traffic, but also to sounds that would usually go unheard. They listen to the silence and try to hear what it sounds like. Or they observe how their body responds to different kinds of music.

These exercises are only suggestions. There are many more ways to sharpen the senses.

## Sense Deprivation for Sense Expansion

Takamatsu Sensei reported that he spent his time of asceticism on a remote mountain. The solitude sharpened his senses so much that he could smell a person approaching. From far away, he could even smell whether it was a man or a woman.

So the senses can also be sharpened by depriving them for a while. Some ninja avoided spices and particularly hot, salty, or bitter foods. That way they noticed poison in a dish at once.

## Live With All Your Senses

For a ninja, it is important to surrender to the senses. Some misguided systems and religions demand self-control and the renunciation of <q>devilish cravings</q> such as good food, sex, or other sensual pleasures. But a fulfilling life includes every aspect that makes up a living being. Our body and senses are part of us. Whoever submits to self-denial, compulsion, or the rejection of pleasure in order to reach a heightened awareness or a higher level is on the wrong path.
