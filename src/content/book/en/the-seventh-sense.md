## Premonition and Intuition

Someone is sitting in a café when another person starts to stare at them. Often the one being stared at feels a kind of unease. The feeling is common and has been studied in experiments for more than a hundred years; so far, none of them has shown that it is a sense of its own.

The ability to sense something before it happens is called the seventh sense, intuition, or premonition. Many people often have such impressions, feelings, and hunches. Most, however, dismiss them and don’t let them in.

A ninja practices refining this perception until they can sense danger and escape it. In the test for _godan_ (五段) in the Bujinkan (武神館), the teacher approaches the student in complete silence with a sword, today a _shinai_ (竹刀). By this stage of training, the student should have sharpened their senses so far that they feel the danger of the sword stroke and avoid it with an evasive movement.

## The Seventh Sense in the Sakki Test

Hatsumi took this test himself, under his master Takamatsu. It is also known as the test of _sakki_ (殺気, <q>killing intent</q>): in the test for fifth dan, the student must dodge a sword without seeing it. By Hatsumi’s own account, he felt pressure on his shoulders and saw in his mind’s eye the image of a hacked-up body. He dodged to the side and passed.

These subtle sensations are said to come from the electromagnetic force fields of other people. Some feel them more strongly, others less. Whoever keeps practicing persistently can go far in this art.
