## In short

This website sets no cookies, embeds no third-party content and loads all fonts and images from its own server. The statistics count visits without recognising you. Your settings (appearance, language, reading position) stay in your browser.

## Controller

The controller under Art. 4 (7) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Stefan Imhoff; address and email are in the [imprint](/en/imprint/).

## Statistics with Umami

To see which pages are read, I use [Umami](https://umami.is/), open-source statistics that I host myself. Umami sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses. It counts the page visited, the referring page, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and country. None of this links a single visit to a person, and the data never leaves my own server. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in improving the book (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

## Fonts

All fonts are served from this website’s server. Your browser makes no connection to Google Fonts or any other font service.

## Storage in the browser

The website remembers in your browser’s storage (`localStorage`, `sessionStorage`) which appearance (light, dark) and which language you chose and where you last read. This information never leaves your browser; it only serves functions you use yourself (§ 25 (2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser’s settings.

## Email

If you write to me, I process your address and your message to reply (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) and delete them once the conversation is over and no obligation to keep them applies.

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). Just send me an email.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live.
